SECTION 04

Operational Zones & Known Locations

ZONE // CLASS-DELTA
The Hypechamber Hub — Orrery Gate
The underground heart of Diesel City. A vast cavern housing a dimensional gate that pulses with blue-white Orrery energy. The S.W.A.T. emblem floats above the balcony observation deck. ALL anomalous entities enter Diesel City through this portal.
Dimensional Flux Orrery Discharge Mass Infiltration Crowd Saturation
ZONE // CLASS-ALPHA
Hypechamber Corridors — Deep Network
The cylindrical tunnel network running beneath Diesel City. Ancient Orrery mandalas inlaid into the floor activate at irregular intervals. The endless perspective creates spatial disorientation. Lantern-posts every 30 meters — do not let them go dark.
Spatial Distortion Mandala Pulse No Retreat
ZONE // CLASS-OMEGA
The Engine Cathedral — Inner Sanctum
An ancient gothic hall housing massive mechanical monoliths. Each pillar is a sealed golem-chamber — breach one and trigger automated deployment. The cathedral hosts the Idol Engine and the Gilded Sentinel. Do not enter without Omega clearance.
Golem Deployment Orrery Surge Zero Communication No Extraction
ZONE // CLASS-BETA
Diesel City Harbor — Western Docks
The fog-shrouded waterway flanked by gothic-industrial towers. Armored submersible vessels operate in the mist. Commander Nyra Solenne holds authority here. Multiple threat vectors from both sea and clocktower vantage points.
Zero Visibility Sub-Surface Threat Multi-Vector
ZONE // CLASS-BETA
The Citadel Spires — Eastern Harbor
The gleaming white spires of the Eastern Citadel rise from the harbor in impossible configurations. A crashed submersible vessel lies embedded in the cliff face — classified hull markings suggest it belonged to the pre-war Orrery fleet. This site is actively contested.
Contested Territory Vertical Combat Structural Instability
ZONE // CLASS-GAMMA
The Pagoda Quarter — Waterway District
Multi-tiered pagoda structures rise from the city's inland waterways. The morning mist here carries trace Orrery particles — prolonged exposure causes mild precognition. Operatives report seeing their own deaths if stationed here more than 72 hours.
Psychic Exposure Orrery Particles 72hr Limit
ZONE // CLASS-ALPHA
The Deep Hype Tunnels — Subsection 9
Narrower tributary tunnels off the main Hypechamber network. Baroque engravings cover every surface — classified as pre-Diesel architecture. Constructs and Oracle Caste members use these as unmarked transit routes. Set detection nodes at every third junction.
Unmarked Routes Low Visibility Unknown Exits
ZONE // CLASS-GAMMA
The Underground Arena — Sector 7
A retrofitted subterranean theater converted into a combat arena. Tiered seating surrounds a central combat ring — illegal Golem fights happen here weekly. Intelligence suggests the MetaHuman Apex was first activated in this facility.
Illegal Operations Golem Combat Hostile Crowd
