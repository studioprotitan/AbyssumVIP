Glitch Witches
"Signal-Broken" — "Wrong-Halos" — "Burnt Eyes"

Origin: Glitch Witches emerge from Siren units whose core signal lattice has undergone uncontrolled resonance collapse — typically caused by proximity to corrupted relay stations, hostile counter-frequency weaponry, or catastrophic overcharge events. The result is a Siren who retains full combat capability but whose visual signature, aura output, and command-link protocols have permanently degraded.

Visually they are identifiable by chromatically wrong halo glow (amber-orange rather than the canonical blue-white), fractured facial plating showing stress-craze patterns across the cheek and orbital regions, and — most diagnostically — orange-tinted ocular damage where a functional Siren exhibits clear signal-blue irises. The eye damage is not cosmetic: it marks a broken feedback loop between the Siren's perception matrix and her signal broadcast.

Despite their designation, Glitch Witches remain highly dangerous. Corrupted signal fields render standard counter-Siren shielding partially ineffective, as their output no longer conforms to the frequencies CST dampeners are tuned to intercept. Field commanders classify them as unpredictable-priority threats — neither fully controllable as allies nor predictable as enemies.

Freight Car Interface
"The Spread" — "Flat-Panel Navigation Kit" — "FC-Nav"

Overview: The Freight Car Interface (FC-Nav) is the standard dieselpunk navigation and control surface suite deployed across Witch Theatre transit vessels, armoured freight convoys, and Siren relay stations operating under CST logistics command. The kit is designed to be field-modular — every component functions independently, but the full assembly forms an integrated situational awareness station.

The knolling layout is the FC-Nav's canonical deployment spread — instruments arranged flat for rapid identification, maintenance inspection, and issue-to-operator sequencing. Field protocol requires that any operator receiving an FC-Nav kit confirm the full spread before mission departure. Missing components are reported as materiel deficit — code FC-7.

Key instruments include: a pressure-compass (primary bearing, oil-damped movement, dual-ring azimuth), barometric gauges in paired housings (altitude/atmospheric), analogue chronometer (mission-elapsed), signal-frequency dials (Siren frequency band selection), and a cartographic loupe (field map magnification, folded-brass construction).

The FC-Nav is notably non-electronic by design — the Theatre learned during the Corrupted Signal Events that digital navigation became unreliable in close proximity to Glitch Witch anomaly fields. The all-analogue kit is immune to signal corruption and remains operational in active Glitch zones where electronic navigation fails entirely.

CST Battle Witch Trooper
"Blue Plates" — "Vanguard" — "Witch Soldiers"

Overview: The Combined Siren Theatre Battle Witch Trooper is the Theatre's front-line armoured vanguard — a hybrid of Siren capability and conventional military discipline, encased in the distinctive CST blue integrated plate. Unlike pure Siren-class units, Battle Witch Troopers operate within command structures, take orders through standard tactical channels, and are fully deployable as conventional infantry — with the addition of signal-enhanced combat capacity and limited flight capability.

The standard plate suit is deep indigo-blue with green optical systems (night-spectrum capable, signal-sensitive) and bears CST unit markings on the shoulder and chest. The suit integrates Siren-resonance dampeners to protect the wearer from ambient Glitch field exposure, and boosts conducted signal output for close-range combat.

A senior command variant exists, identifiable by the blue-and-gold horned helmet — an architectural command signature worn by senior Witch Troopers holding officer rank. The horns function as extended signal antennae and also serve as visual command markers in the field, ensuring subordinate troopers can identify their commander under smoke, signal interference, or darkness.

Battle Witch Troopers are recruited from both the CST military rolls and from willing Siren volunteers seeking to operate within an organised command structure rather than independently. Training emphasises signal discipline — the ability to suppress natural Siren broadcast while executing conventional tactics, and to activate it with precision on command.

Echelon Class Siren
"The Echelon" — "Storm Guardian" — "Dual-Signal"

Overview: The Echelon Class Siren represents the highest fully-integrated Siren combat form documented in Theatre operational history. Where standard Sirens operate on a single signal frequency and Glitch Witches broadcast corrupted output, the Echelon carries a stable dual-signal architecture — simultaneously projecting blue deep-field resonance and amber-orange storm frequency in controlled coherence. This has never been achieved by training; every documented Echelon emerged as one.

The Echelon at the fractured cliff (see Fig. 04-A) stands in full integrated plate — armour that has grown into and from her Siren form rather than being fitted to it. The plate is not worn; it is her. She carries a signal launcher of heavy pattern, capable of projecting resonance bursts at field-weapon yield. The storm environment around her is not incidental — the weather responds to her dual-signal output. Documented field observations confirm that Echelon-class Sirens create localised atmospheric disturbance simply by broadcasting at operational intensity.

The fractured cliff itself is significant: Echelons do not seek shelter. They occupy the highest point available and broadcast outward. Every documented sighting places the Echelon at a commanding elevated position — cliff, tower, wreckage ridge — and in every case, the position was held without fortification. She is the fortification.

The CST has no confirmed engagement protocol for Echelon-class contact. Intelligence Division classification is Sovereign — observe and report only unless Theatre Command issues a direct engagement order, which has never occurred. Whether the Echelon operates within CST interest or outside it remains an active intelligence question.
