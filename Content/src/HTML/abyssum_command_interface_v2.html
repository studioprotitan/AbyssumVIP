SIREN WITCH FORMATION — PRE-DIESEL CITY
The six variants predate the Abyssum command structure. Their formation is tied to The Forge itself — an event before the city was built. Variant V (Commander) is the oldest active field unit in any Abyssum theater. Her armor aesthetic is pre-industrial, suggesting the Forge was accessed or weaponized before Diesel City codified it as infrastructure. The team was not recruited by Abyssum — they were absorbed. The terms of that absorption are classified above Omega-1 clearance.
CORESTONE AFFINITY — HYBRID CLASSIFICATION
Unlike standard Golem units whose Corestone alignment is fixed at fabrication, Siren Witch variants carry live Corestones as field-switchable equipment. Variant I's belt shows four active stones in rotation. This suggests an adaptive combat doctrine not seen in any other unit class. Corestone assignments per variant are pending full field assessment.
