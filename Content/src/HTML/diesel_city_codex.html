WORLD LORE

THE UNDERGROUND — DIESEL CITY

Beneath the smog-choked surface streets lies the true city — a subterranean megastructure of arcane-industrial engineering spanning hundreds of kilometers of tunnels, vaulted chambers, and engine-halls. Diesel City was built around the Golem Power Cores — ancient arcane reactors that convert dimensional energy into usable force. The city grew outward from these Cores like rings around a tree. Everything runs on Core energy: the lights, the vehicles, the weapons, the witches.

THE ASH — ORIGIN OF THE WITCHES

Long before Diesel City was fortified, the first Core overloaded. The explosion of raw arcane energy — known as The Ash Event — transformed a generation of survivors. Those women who absorbed the Ash without dying became the first Ash-Born Witches. The Ash rewrote their biology: enhanced perception, arcane attunement, and the ability to interface directly with Core technology. The S.W.A.T. program was built around them. Every Witch carries Ash in her cells. It is both her power and her curse.

THE GLITCH — CORRUPTION OF THE GREEN

Some Ash-Born did not align with S.W.A.T. They went deeper — studying the Ash not as a tool but as a living system. These practitioners discovered that Ash energy, left unregulated, begins to merge with organic matter — growing through bone, animating moss, dissolving the boundary between the living and the dead. They called this process The Glitch. Those consumed by it became the Glitch Witches — still recognizably Ash-Born (the natural afro is the ancestral marker that never disappears), but transformed into architects of life-death duality magic. Their green bioluminescence is Ash energy running wild through exposed bone.

THE RED SCHISM — BLOOD COVENANT

A second divergence from the Ash-Born line occurred when a group of Glitch Witches attempted to weaponize the Glitch against the Rift itself. The experiment failed — catastrophically. The Rift energy inverted their Glitch corruption, turning green bioluminescence to crimson and accelerating vampiric decay. These Blood Covenant Witches retain some ancestral afro hair but their faces are stripped to white bone, their eyes bleed red light, and their power is predatory rather than cultivating. They are faster than Glitch Witches, less rooted, and infinitely more dangerous in direct combat. The moss they stand on is not their ally — it is the last trace of what they used to be.

THE RIFT — DIMENSIONAL INVASION

The Cores do not generate energy — they extract it from adjacent dimensions. For centuries this process was stable. Then something on the other side began pushing back. The Rift-Born are the inhabitants of those dimensions — beings of pale teal energy and exposed bone, armored in scale-that-grew-from-skin, who enter through unstable Core extraction points. They do not invade out of malice. They invade because from their perspective, the Cores are consuming their world. The tragedy of Diesel City is that both sides have legitimate grievances. S.W.A.T. fights to survive. The Rift-Born fight to reclaim. Purple energy is volatile Rift matter — explosive and dimensionally unstable on contact with Core-attuned technology.

// FILE 002 — FACTION INTELLIGENCE

FACTIONS

⊕
S.W.A.T. WITCHES
ALIGNMENT: DEFENDER // ARCANE TACTICAL

Elite arcane-tactical operatives of Diesel City. Each Witch is Ash-Born — a survivor of the original Core event who carries the Ash in her biology. S.W.A.T. Witches are simultaneously combat medics, field alchemists, and living weapons systems. Their four-vial belt system carries elemental alchemical compounds keyed to earth (green), fire (red), water (blue), and lightning (yellow). The S.W.A.T. designation is permanently inscribed in their armor in teal — both identification and ward.

ARCANE-TACTICAL ASH-BORN FIELD ALCHEMIST CORE-ATTUNED
◈
THE SILVER SENTINEL
ALIGNMENT: COMMAND // ADMINISTRATIVE

The masked Director of Diesel City. The Sentinel operates from a gold-bronze throne room at the city's administrative core — surrounded by the machinery of governance itself. The featureless silver mask conceals identity, rendering the Director a symbol rather than a person. Commands S.W.A.T., CST, and all city operations. The silver domino and chain-cape are walking archives of inscribed city law. The Sentinel sees all and says little.

COMMAND TIER ADMINISTRATIVE UNKNOWN ORIGIN
⬡
CST OPERATIVES
ALIGNMENT: LAW ENFORCEMENT // SURFACE

City Surface Tactical — the ground-level law enforcement corps patrolling the above-ground districts of Diesel City. Where S.W.A.T. handles Rift-level threats underground, CST maintains order on the burning surface streets. Their horned tactical helmets and orange eye-lenses indicate augmented-vision implants tuned to a different frequency than Core energy. CST operatives are potentially the guardians of the Power Core access points at surface level.

SURFACE OPS AUGMENTED CORE GUARDIAN
✦
ASH-BORN RELIC MYSTIC
ALIGNMENT: ALLIED // ARCANE KNOWLEDGE

The oldest living Ash-Born operative. The Relic Mystic predates S.W.A.T. — he was present at the original Ash Event and has been studying its implications for generations. His alchemy wheel interface projects twelve nodes simultaneously, each representing a force in the elemental catalogue. His single blue eye sees the Rift directly. His mechanical rifle is larger than his body and tuned to dimensional frequencies. He knows things about the Rift-Born that S.W.A.T. command refuses to acknowledge.

ANCIENT RIFT-SIGHTED 12-NODE ALCHEMY
⊗
KRAKEN PILOTS
ALIGNMENT: ALLIED // NAVIGATION & DEEP FIELD

A non-human species of cephalopod-biomechanical beings — dome-skulled, tentacle-faced, armored in glacial teal plating threaded with gold. Kraken Pilots are the navigators, helmsmen, and deep-field operators of Diesel City's outlying operations. They pilot the Kraken Vehicle — a dome-shelled, building-sized land crawler — through Rift-adjacent terrain that would destroy conventional S.W.A.T. vehicles. Their goggles see in spectrums beyond human range.

NON-HUMAN DEEP FIELD NAVIGATOR CLASS
☠
GREEN GLITCH WITCHES
ALIGNMENT: ANTAGONIST // BIO-CORRUPTION

Ash-Born who surrendered to the Glitch — unregulated Ash energy growing through their bone, lighting them green from within. They cultivate and corrupt slowly, like disease. The natural afro is their undeniable ancestral marker — they cannot shed their lineage even in death. They are rooted: the moss at their feet grows thicker the longer they hold a location, until they become part of the landscape. They don't attack cities — they become them.

ROOTED BIO-CORRUPTION GLITCH-CONSUMED ASH LINEAGE
✸
RED BLOOD COVENANT
ALIGNMENT: ANTAGONIST // BLOOD MAGIC

Glitch Witches who attempted to weaponize the Glitch against the Rift and paid the price. Rift energy inverted their corruption: green became crimson, cultivation became predation, roots became fangs. Blood Covenant Witches are the fastest and most volatile of all antagonist factions. They hunt rather than spread. Their white-bone faces bleed red light. The moss at their feet is the last ghost of their former nature. Some retain the ancestral afro — the bridge between factions that S.W.A.T. intelligence has never fully explained.

PREDATORY VAMPIRIC RIFT-INVERTED MOBILE
⬟
THE RIFT-BORN
ALIGNMENT: ANTAGONIST // DIMENSIONAL

Inhabitants of the dimensions being drained by the Golem Power Cores. They enter Diesel City through unstable extraction points — dimensional tears disguised as portal arches. Rift-Born armor grows from their bodies; it is not worn. Their hierarchy ranges from shock-troops to storm-callers to named divine entities of terrifying power. The tragedy: they are not evil. They are defending their home from a city that has been consuming their world for centuries.

ARCANE GOLEM POWER CORE

CLASSIFICATION: ARCANE-INDUSTRIAL REACTOR

The Golem Power Core is the engine of Diesel City — a spherical arcane-industrial reactor that extracts dimensional energy from adjacent planes and converts it into usable force. The outer orange rings process raw dimensional intake. The inner blue eye is the stable Core consciousness — a semi-sentient monitoring system that has been running since the city's founding. Everything in Diesel City runs on Core energy: lights, vehicles, weapons, the Witches themselves.
