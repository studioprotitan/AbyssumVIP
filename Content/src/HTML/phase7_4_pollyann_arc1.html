Who Polly Ann Is

In the old record — the ballad version, the version sung at the end of long work — Polly Ann drove steel. Not after John Henry died to honor him. Because the spike needed to go in and someone had to hold the hammer and she was there and she knew how.

That detail gets lost. The story becomes about John Henry racing the steam drill, about a man’s pride against a machine’s arithmetic. What doesn’t get sung as often: when he fell, the work continued. The rail went forward. The mountain did not stop being a mountain.

“Polly Ann drove steel like a man.
Lord, Lord.
Polly Ann drove steel like a man.” — Traditional · John Henry Ballad · Verse disputed

In Diesel City, the myth is old enough to have calcified. The John Henry Core — the engine district built above the rail tunnels where the original contest happened — is a monument now. Statues. Plaques. A museum that charges admission. The steam drill is displayed behind glass.

No one put up a statue of Polly Ann. No one thought to. She kept driving steel after the contest ended and no one was watching and there was no race to win. That kind of work doesn’t get bronze.

She arrives in the Genesis Verse as a Pilot — which is the present-tense word for someone who has descended far enough into Diesel City’s infrastructure to matter. She does not arrive with a mission statement. She arrives with a look on her face that says she has seen the museum, and she has seen the display case with the steam drill, and she has a thought about that.

Arc 1 — The Steel Heir · What’s Inside The Mountain

The cinematic establishes her presence. Arc 1 — across subsequent phases — follows her through the four theatres in a sequence the game does not mandate but the story does. She is not a completionist. She is not collecting cards. She wants to find out what happened at the bottom of the original contest, and Diesel City has been building on top of that answer for a very long time.

Arc 1 Story Beats — Phases 7.5 → 7.8
The Steel Heir — Outline
Phase 7.5 · The Deck · Polly Ann clears the first arena. The NPCs don’t know who she is. She doesn’t correct them.
Phase 7.6 · The Canopy · She finds a boundary stone with script she recognizes. Pre-linguistic. Same script as the name “Abyssum.” She was here before, in another form.
Phase 7.7 · The Substrate · The 7 Substrate NPCs have a name for her in their own language. They don’t say it out loud. She notices.
Phase 7.8 · The Abyssum · She stands where the Terror King was. The floor is still warm. She drives her hammer into it once. The sound it makes is the sound the mountain made when John Henry’s spike went in for the last time. She turns around and walks back out.
Arc 1 Resolution · Not a boss fight. Not a card drop. A choice. The game offers it in plain text with no timer.
